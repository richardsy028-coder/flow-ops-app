BUSINESS TERMS SECTION Add simple explanations: KPI: A KPI is a number you track to know if your business is improving. Example: sales, inquiries, conversion rate, or response time. SOP: An SOP is a step-by-step guide for doing a task correctly. Example: how to reply to inquiries, process orders, or send reports. Workflow: A workflow is the path a task follows from start to finish. Example: inquiry → reply → offer → payment → delivery → follow-up. Bottleneck: A bottleneck is the part of the business that slows everything down. Example: delayed follow-ups or unclear task ownership. Revenue leak: A revenue leak is where potential money is lost. Example: interested customers who never receive a follow-up.
